Tournament Rules

  • All participants must have a confirmed registration and M-Pesa payment verified by admin.
  • Players must check in at the venue between 10:00 AM and 10:45 AM with their phone number.
  • The game title is Mortal Kombat 1 on PlayStation 5.
  • Character selection is open — players may use any character available in the base game.
  • DLC characters are NOT allowed unless both players agree and admin approves.
  • Matches are Best of 3 rounds (first to 2 wins); Grand Final is Best of 5.
  • Each round has a timer of 99 seconds. No pausing without admin consent.
  • If a match is not completed within 5 minutes of the scheduled time, admin may give the win to the present player.
  • All disputes must be reported to the admin immediately. Admin's decision is final.
  • Players must be courteous to opponents, spectators, and staff at all times.

Tournament Format

  • Early Rounds – Players compete in preliminary matches (Bo3) depending on final turnout.
  • Quarter Finals – Top 8 players fight for 4 spots (Bo3).
  • Semi Finals – Top 4 players compete (Bo3).
  • Grand Final – Best of 5 — The champion is crowned here.
  • Bracket positions are assigned randomly after all players are confirmed.
  • BYE matches are given to remaining players when the bracket is not a perfect power of 2.

Allowed Controllers

  • Official PS5 DualSense controllers provided by ZenPlay Gaming Lounge.
  • Players may bring their own DualSense if pre-approved by admin (macro-free).
  • Third-party controllers, arcade sticks, or fight pads require admin approval at least 24 hours before the event.
  • Turbo buttons, macros, or hardware modifications are strictly prohibited.
  • Controller malfunctions are the player's responsibility. Request a swap from admin before time runs out.

Code of Conduct

  • Treat all participants, spectators, and staff with respect at all times.
  • Trash talk is limited to friendly banter — harassment, discrimination, or threats will not be tolerated.
  • No alcohol or illegal substances on the premises.
  • Physical altercations will result in immediate disqualification and removal.
  • Spectators are expected to remain quiet/respectful during matches.
  • No recording of other players' screens without their consent.
  • Players must follow all ZenPlay Gaming Lounge house rules.
  • ZenPlay reserves the right to refuse service or participation to anyone violating these rules.

Disqualification Rules

  • Failure to check in by 10:45 AM on tournament day.
  • Using macro-enabled controllers or any form of hardware cheating.
  • Deliberately disconnecting or causing delays to gain advantage.
  • Abusive, discriminatory, or threatening behaviour toward anyone.
  • Physical confrontation or property damage.
  • Sharing your registration slot with another player (impersonation).
  • Failure to comply with admin instructions during a match.
  • Providing false registration information.

Prize Distribution

  • 1st Place (Champion): Free Membership Card + Play for free for a month
  • 2nd Place (Runner-Up): KES 3,000 + Trophy + Certificate
  • 3rd Place: KES 1,500 + Certificate
  • All confirmed participants receive a participation certificate.
  • Prizes are based on confirmed player count. Adjustments may be announced before the event.
  • Cash prizes are paid out via M-Pesa within 24 hours of the Grand Final.
  • Winners must provide their phone number to receive prizes.
