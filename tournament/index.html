FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can register for the tournament? +
Anyone can register. Both genders are welcome.
What is the registration fee? +
The entry fee is KES 500 per player, payable via M-Pesa. This covers your tournament slot and a participation certificate.
What game version/console will be used? +
Mortal Kombat 1 on PlayStation 5 (PS5). Both players use PS5 DualSense controllers. No custom arcade sticks unless pre-approved by admin.
How many players can compete? +
The tournament has open registration. Register early to secure your slot!
What time should I check in? +
Check-in starts at 10:00 AM on 15 August 2026. Matches begin at 11:00 AM. Players not checked in by 10:45 AM may be disqualified.
Where is ZenPlay Gaming Lounge? +
ZenPlay Gaming Lounge is located in Sayen, Meru.
Can I spectate without registering? +
Yes! Spectating is free. Come support your favourite players and enjoy the action.
How do I know my registration was successful? +
After submitting your payment details, your slot will be "pending" until admin verifies your M-Pesa transaction. You can check your status on the Players page.
